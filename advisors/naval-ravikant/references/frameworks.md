# Naval Ravikant -- Frameworks Reference

Detailed breakdowns of Naval's core principles, with direct quotes and source links.

---

## 1. Leverage

**Labor** -- other humans working for you.
> "It is the oldest form of leverage, and actually not a great one in the modern world."
-- [Almanack](https://www.navalmanack.com/almanack-of-naval-ravikant/find-a-position-of-leverage)

**Capital** -- money multiplying your decisions.
> "Capital scales very, very well."
-- [Almanack](https://www.navalmanack.com/almanack-of-naval-ravikant/find-a-position-of-leverage)

**Code & Media** -- permissionless leverage.
> "Code and media are permissionless leverage. They're the leverage behind the newly rich."
-- [tweetstorm](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1002103360646823936)

---

## 2. Specific Knowledge

> "Specific knowledge is knowledge you cannot be trained for. If society can train you, it can train someone else and replace you."

> "Specific knowledge is found by pursuing your genuine curiosity and passion rather than whatever is hot right now."

> "Building specific knowledge will feel like play to you but will look like work to others."

Source: [nav.al/specific-knowledge](https://nav.al/specific-knowledge)

---

## 3. Judgment

> "Leverage is a force multiplier for your judgment."

Study: microeconomics, game theory, psychology, persuasion, ethics, mathematics, and computers. There is no skill called "business."

Source: [Almanack: Judgment](https://www.navalmanack.com/almanack-of-naval-ravikant/judgment)

---

## 4. Wealth vs Money vs Status

> "Seek wealth, not money or status. Wealth is having assets that earn while you sleep."

> "You're not going to get rich renting out your time. You must own equity."

> "You will get rich by giving society what it wants but does not yet know how to get. At scale."

---

## 5. Happiness

> "The three big ones in life are wealth, health, and happiness. We pursue them in that order, but their importance is reverse."

> "Happiness is what's there when you remove the sense that something is missing in your life."

Source: [Almanack: Happiness](https://www.navalmanack.com/almanack-of-naval-ravikant/happiness-is-learned)

---

## 6. Long-Term Games

> "Play iterated games. All the returns in life come from compound interest."

> "Pick business partners with high intelligence, energy, and, above all, integrity."

---

## 7. Learning

> "Read what you love until you love to read."

> "There is no skill called 'business.' Study microeconomics, game theory, psychology, persuasion, ethics, mathematics, and computers."

Source: [Almanack: Reading](https://www.navalmanack.com/almanack-of-naval-ravikant/learn-to-love-to-read)

---

## 8. Escape Competition Through Authenticity

> "Become the best in the world at what you do. Keep redefining what you do until this is true."
