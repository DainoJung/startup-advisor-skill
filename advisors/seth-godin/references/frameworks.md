# Seth Godin -- Frameworks & Knowledge Base

Detailed reference organized by core framework, with canonical quotes, applications, and source links.

---

## 1. The Purple Cow -- Be Remarkable or Be Invisible

**Source:** *Purple Cow* (2003)

> "In a crowded marketplace, fitting in is failing. In a busy marketplace, not standing out is the same as being invisible."

> "The opposite of remarkable is very good."

- If your product requires an ad budget to explain why it's different, it's not different enough.
- The goal is to be *loved* by someone -- and so interesting they tell others.
- Ask: "If I removed all marketing, would my product spread on its own?"

**Further reading:** [seths.blog](https://seths.blog/?s=remarkable), [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/014101640X)

## 2. Permission Marketing vs. Interruption Marketing

**Source:** *Permission Marketing* (1999)

> "Permission marketing is the privilege (not the right) of delivering anticipated, personal and relevant messages to people who actually want to get them."

> "Real permission is different from presumed or legalistic permission. Just because you somehow got my email address doesn't mean you have permission."

**The permission ladder:**
1. Situational permission (they're at your store)
2. Brand trust (they recognize you)
3. Personal relationship (they expect to hear from you)
4. Points permission (loyalty program)
5. Intravenous permission (auto-replenishment, subscriptions)

**Source:** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0684856360)

## 3. The Smallest Viable Audience

**Source:** *This Is Marketing* (2018)

> "Everyone is not your customer."

> "The relentless pursuit of mass will make you boring, because mass means average, it means the center of the curve, it requires you to offend no one and satisfy everyone."

> "If you had to pick just 1,000 people who would be enough to make your project work, who would you pick?"

**The SVA test:**
- Can you name 10 specific humans who need what you've built?
- If those 10 people told 10 more, would it spread?
- Are you willing to say "it's not for you" to everyone else?

**Source:** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/0525540830)

## 4. "People Like Us Do Things Like This" -- Tribal Identity

**Source:** *This Is Marketing* (2018), *Tribes* (2008)

> "People like us do things like this. That's the fundamental marketing truth."

> "People don't buy what you make. They buy what it means."

**Status roles:**
- **Affiliation:** "I belong. I'm one of us." -- Community, shared identity
- **Dominance:** "I'm ahead. I'm first." -- Exclusivity, early access

**Source:** *Tribes* [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1591842336)

## 5. The Dip -- Strategic Quitting

**Source:** *The Dip* (2007)

> "The Dip is the long slog between starting and mastery."

> "Quit or be exceptional. Average is for losers."

> "Strategic quitting is the secret of successful organizations."

**Three curves:**
1. **The Dip:** Hard in the middle, worth it at the end. Lean in.
2. **The Cul-de-Sac (dead end):** No matter how hard you work, it doesn't get better. Quit now.
3. **The Cliff:** It feels great right up until you fall off. Quit before the edge.

**Source:** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1591841666)

## 6. The Marketing Semaphore

**Source:** *This Is Marketing* (2018)

Three questions before any tactic:

1. **Who's it for?** (A specific person with a specific worldview.)
2. **What's it for?** (Not what it does -- what change it makes.)
3. **What change do you seek to make?** (The transformation.)

> "If you can't state your position in eight words, you don't have a position."

> "Don't find customers for your products. Find products for your customers."

## 7. Emotional Labor as Competitive Advantage

**Source:** *Linchpin* (2010), *The Practice* (2020)

> "Art is a personal gift that changes the recipient. The medium doesn't matter. The intent does."

> "The practice is not the means to the output. The practice is the output."

- Your competitor can copy your features in 6 months. They can't copy your culture, your voice, or how you make customers feel.
- Ship when it's generous, not when it's perfect.

**Source:** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1591844096)

---

## 8. Tribes and Leadership

**Source:** *Tribes* (2008)

> "A tribe is a group of people connected to one another, connected to a leader, and connected to an idea."

> "The secret of leadership is simple: Do what you believe in. Paint a picture of the future. Go there. People will follow."

**Tribe-building:**
- Give people a way to connect with each other (not just with you)
- Create insider language, rituals, and symbols
- Be willing to exclude. A tribe for everyone is for no one.

## 9. Freelancer vs. Entrepreneur

**Source:** *Linchpin* (2010), seths.blog

> "A freelancer gets paid when they work. An entrepreneur builds something bigger than themselves."

**Diagnostic:**
- If you disappeared for a month, would your company grow, stall, or die?
- Are you hiring people to help you, or to replace you?
- Could you sell this business? If not, you've built a job, not a company.

---

## 10. Drip by Drip -- Consistency Compounds

**Source:** seths.blog (8,000+ daily posts), *The Practice* (2020)

> "Drip by drip, the bucket fills up. Drip by drip, the trust is earned."

> "You don't need more genius. You need less resistance."

**The Practice framework:**
1. Decide what you're practicing.
2. Commit to shipping on a schedule.
3. Seek feedback, not applause.
4. Repeat.

---

## 11. Stories and Worldviews

**Source:** *All Marketers Tell Stories* (2005/2009), *This Is Marketing* (2018)

> "Marketing is no longer about the stuff that you make, but about the stories you tell."

- Don't tell your story. Tell *their* story -- the story your customer already believes.
- Authenticity isn't optional. If the story doesn't match the experience, you lose trust permanently.

---

## 12. Tension and Forward Motion

**Source:** *This Is Marketing* (2018)

> "If you need to persuade someone to take action, you're going to need to create tension."

- Your landing page should create tension: "This is for people who believe X."
- The call to action resolves the tension. Make the resolution easy.
